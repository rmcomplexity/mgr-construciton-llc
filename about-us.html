About Us

MGR Construction LLC — Building your vision exactly how you want it, within budget, with complete transparency at every step.

Our Story

Why We're Different

Worried about construction projects where crews disappear, the project goes out of budget or takes longer than expected to finish, or projects where it's not clear what's behind the walls?

We can help build your vision exactly how you want it within budget and with a clear timeline. We created a process where customer communication and transparency is front and center. No more stressing about what is going on in the project or if all the infrastructure work (what's left behind the walls) was done correctly.

We deliver clear documentation of every step done and keep you in the loop with daily (optional) and weekly updates and reports.

Common Industry Problems

  • Crews disappear mid-project with no communication
  • Projects go over budget with surprise costs
  • Timelines stretch far beyond original estimates
  • No visibility into infrastructure work behind walls
  • Lack of documentation and accountability

The MGR Construction Way

  • Dedicated project manager as your single point of contact
  • Clear budget with milestone-based payments
  • Defined timeline with weekly progress reports
  • Complete photo documentation including behind-wall work
  • Daily optional updates and full transparency

Our Core Values

Every project we take on is guided by these principles.

Transparency

Complete visibility into every aspect of your project — budget, timeline, and workmanship. No hidden costs, no surprises.

Communication

Daily optional check-ins and mandatory weekly reports ensure you always know exactly where your project stands.

Quality

Integrity

Accountability

We offer a 2-year warranty on the labor for all of our projects. This isn't just a promise — it's our guarantee that we stand behind every nail, every joint, and every finish.
