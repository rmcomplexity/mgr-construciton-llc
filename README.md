3. Our Process

    HTML mockup in `file:docs/mockups/our-process.html`

    1. Initial Consultation: Meet with your dedicated project manager to discuss your vision, requirements, and goals. We'll review site conditions and gather all necessary documentation including blueprints and permits.
    2. Detailed Proposal: Receive a comprehensive budget breakdown and timeline with clearly marked milestones and deliverables. No hidden fees, no surprises—just complete transparency.
    3. Active Construction: Daily 15-minute optional updates and mandatory weekly progress reports keep you informed. We address concerns immediately and adapt to any changes professionally.
    4. Milestone Payments: Pay only when milestones are completed and reviewed. This ensures quality at every stage and keeps the project moving forward with confidence.
    5. Project Completion: Receive comprehensive documentation including before, during, and after photos—even of work hidden behind walls. Plus detailed warranty coverage explanation.
